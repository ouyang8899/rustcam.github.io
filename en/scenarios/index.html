RustCam

Scenarios

How RustCam is applied to campus security, city operations, industrial safety, mobile patrol and transport hubs: pain points, capability mapping and measurable outcomes.

Campus security

Cameras from different vendors are scattered across risers and floors; alarms rely on manual patrol and incidents take minutes to locate.

▸Incident location drops from minutes to seconds; new devices only need configuration, no extra development.

Capability mapping

  • ·Unified GB28181 / ONVIF / RTSP ingestion
  • ·Electronic map with clustered markers
  • ·Video wall layout with drag ordering
  • ·Recording policies per zone and time window

City operations

Regional video resources are split across district platforms; cross-domain access needs manual approval and permission boundaries are blurry.

▸Cross-domain access goes online, permissions are centrally governed, and every action is auditable.

Capability mapping

  • ·GB28181 cascading and platform broadcast
  • ·Users / roles / permissions model
  • ·JWT + Casbin fine-grained authorisation
  • ·Administrative regions and device groups

Industrial safety

Production floors and offices share permissions, alarms have no closed loop, and responsibility cannot be traced.

▸Every alarm is traceable from occurrence to resolution, with a complete chain for later evidence.

Capability mapping

  • ·Alarm intake and processing status
  • ·Per-channel status monitoring
  • ·Exception recording policies and playback
  • ·Local live preview on the desktop client

Mobile patrol (Mobile Eye)

Blind spots and temporary deployments beyond fixed points are handled manually; patrol records and evidence end up on paper and personal devices.

▸Fly there when it is needed; patrol and evidence stay inside the platform instead of spawning a second system.

Capability mapping

  • ·Drone flight-control integration (MAVLink / PX4 / ArduPilot)
  • ·Live position and attitude on a Cesium 3D globe
  • ·Route planning, saving and sequential execution
  • ·Geofences with breach alarms and command blocking
  • ·Drones link platform cameras; channels resolve live and stream automatically
  • ·Control leases plus flight-log audit

Transport hubs

Concurrent channels spike during rush hours; live streams and recording tasks compete for bandwidth.

▸Capacity scales linearly with the streaming cluster and a single point of failure no longer interrupts service.

Capability mapping

  • ·Weighted multi-server routing with failover
  • ·Clustered Media Server deployment
  • ·TLS protected transport (HTTPS / WSS)
  • ·Dual TCP / UDP transport modes
